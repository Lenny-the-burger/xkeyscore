Tech Extractor Limitations a

e While terms tasked for the Tech Extractor are applied
only to Document, E-mail and Chat bodies, that is still
a lot of traffic!

e If the term is too generic (or short) you're still likely to
run into a lot of false hits.

e Also, while you can limit your results by adding more
search criteria (country code, IP address etc), the term
will be scanning all data looking for hits

Tech Extractor vs. Fingerprints

e Tech Extractor treats E-mail, Chat and Document
bodies as a single “context”

e The XKS Fingerprint language gives you over 65+
contexts that can be used together to form powerful
and specific signatures

e When terms are generic and are returning too many
poor results through Tech Extractor, then it’s time to
make the switch to the full fingerprint language
